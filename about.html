The problem

Most parents mean to pray for their children. In practice it happens in snatches — reactively, when something is wrong, and usually about the same two or three things: a fever, a hard week at school, a worry that won't settle. The rest of a child's life — their courage, their friendships, who they are becoming, the faith you hope takes root — isn't left out on purpose. It just rarely makes it into words.

What the app does about it

Over Their Lives is being built to turn praying for your children into something you can actually keep up — prayer that is intentional (chosen, rather than improvised at the end of a long day), consistent (a rhythm you return to, not only a response to a crisis) and holistic (across body, soul and spirit: your child's health and protection, their mind and emotions and friendships, and their faith and identity — not only their safety and their grades).

Every prayer is chosen for the child in front of you. You add each child with a name and date of birth, and the prayers fit their age, so the same theme is prayed differently for a toddler than for a sixteen-year-old.

What we hope to achieve

That a parent can reach the end of a year having prayed across the whole of their child's life, rather than the same corner of it. And that years of those prayers become something a family can look back on — a record of having stood over a child's life in prayer, season after season.

We do not promise outcomes. What we can offer is a way to be faithful and unhurried about something that matters to you.
