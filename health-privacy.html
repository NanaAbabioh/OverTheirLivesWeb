Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Over Their Lives

5 min readUpdated 23 November 2026

This policy is for residents of Washington State and Nevada, and for anyone whose health information we handle in those states. It is required by Washington's My Health My Data Act and Nevada's consumer health data law, and it adds to our Privacy Policy. We apply the same protections to everyone, wherever they live.

Over Their Lives is a prayer app, not a health app. It does not diagnose, treat or track any condition. But some of its prayers are about health and wellbeing, and a record of which of those a parent prayed for a named child could say something about that child's health. This policy covers that record.

1. The health data we collect

We collect consumer health data in only two ways.

A record of which health and wellbeing prayers you prayed, if you turn that on. These are the prayers about:

  • physical illness, injury and long-term conditions
  • learning challenges
  • depression, fear, managing emotions and healing from trauma
  • addiction and harmful substances
  • protection from abuse
  • puberty and body changes, and sexual purity

If you turn on Keep a record of health and wellbeing prayers, we keep which of these prayers you prayed, for which child, and when. We also keep any of them you save, and any seven-day week you begin on one of these topics.

If you leave it off, we record only that you prayed, not which of these topics it was.

What you choose to write. If you write about someone's health in your Journal or in a note after a prayer, that text is stored with your account like everything else you write. We do not read it, search it or sort it by topic.

We do not collect health data from your phone, from Apple Health or Google Health Connect, from wearables, from your location, or from any other company.

2. Where it comes from

Only from you: from the prayers you pray in the app and the words you type into it.

3. Why we collect it and how we use it

For one purpose: to show it back to you. It lets the app show your prayer history, your saved prayers and your place in a week you started.

We may also count these prayers in totals across all accounts, for example how often a topic is prayed, and only in totals large enough that no family could be picked out.

We do not use consumer health data for advertising, we do not use it to build a profile of you or your child, and we do not use it to make decisions about you.

We collect the record of health and wellbeing prayers only with your consent, which is a separate, optional choice when you create your account.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell consumer health data. We do not share it with advertisers, data brokers, insurers, employers, or any other third party.

Nobody in your circle sees it. People you invite cannot see any of your prayers. If one of them prays a health and wellbeing prayer for your child, you see only that "a prayer" was prayed, not which one.

It is held for us by one company acting on our instructions:

WhoWhat they doWhere
Supabase (Supabase, Inc.)Hosts our database, where this record and your writing are stored, encrypted.United States (Oregon)

The other companies named in our Privacy Policy do not receive it. The app removes anything that points to a particular prayer or topic before a crash report is sent; the record of how the app is used never includes which prayer you prayed, and Apple, Google and RevenueCat receive nothing about your prayers.

We have no affiliates that receive consumer health data.

We may disclose it if a law or a court order requires us to. We would tell you first where the law allows.

We do not use geofencing, and we never collect your location.

5. Your rights and how to use them

To withdraw your consent: turn off Settings → Privacy → Keep a record of health and wellbeing prayers. The topics already recorded are deleted, along with any saved prayers and weeks on those topics. The rest of the app works as before.

To delete it: the switch above deletes the record of health and wellbeing prayers. You can delete anything you wrote from your Journal. Settings → Account → Delete my account deletes everything. Deleted information leaves our encrypted backups within 7 days.

To confirm what we hold and see a copy: your history, saved prayers, and Journal are in the app. For a copy, or for the list of companies above with their contact details, write to privacy@overtheirlives.com.

If you cannot use the app, write to privacy@overtheirlives.com or use overtheirlives.com/delete-account. We may need to check that you are who you say you are. We answer within 45 days. There is no charge.

If we decline a request, you may appeal by replying to our answer, or by writing to privacy@overtheirlives.com with "Appeal" in the subject. We answer appeals within 45 days and explain our decision in writing. If you are still not satisfied:

  • Washington residents may contact the Washington State Attorney General at atg.wa.gov/file-complaint.
  • Nevada residents may contact the Nevada Attorney General at ag.nv.gov.

We will not treat you differently for using any of these rights.

6. Changes

If we change this policy we will post the new version here and change the date at the top. If we ever wanted to collect or share consumer health data in a new way, we would ask for your consent first.
